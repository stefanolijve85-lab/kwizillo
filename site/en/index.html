What is Kwizillo

A children's encyclopedia you play

Kwizillo wraps knowledge about space, animals, Earth, history, science and mysteries into a quiz adventure children open by themselves — not because they have to, but because it is fun.

🌍

6 worlds, 24 topics

From the solar system to knights and castles, from the human body to treasure hunts. Every world has four topics and a mixed quiz.

🖼️

240 questions, 240 illustrations

Every question has its own picture that shows the subject — never the answer. 240 questions per language, each with a hint, an explanation and a fun fact.

🔊

Milo & Luna read aloud

Question, answers, explanation and fact are read out naturally. Pre-schoolers can play too, and older children practise listening.

🧠

Memo & Maths

Two extra games: Memo with pictures from the worlds and Maths with sums that get harder at every level.

How to play

How a round of Kwizillo works

A quiz is ten questions. Each has four answers, a hint and an explanation — and a timer that follows your level.

1

Pick a world

Six worlds, each with four topics or a mixed quiz.

2

Listen and choose

Milo or Luna reads the question and answers A to D. Not sure? Ask for a hint.

3

Learn from every answer

Right or wrong: you hear the correct answer, a short explanation and a fun fact. Confetti when you are right!

4

Collect and grow

Every correct question becomes a knowledge card. Earn XP, coins, medals and new buddies.

Why it teaches

Every answer is a moment to learn

Kwizillo is built on what works: short, visual, explained, repeated — and rewarded in a way children want to earn themselves.

Explanation instead of just right or wrong

  • After every question your child hears the right answer and why it is right.
  • A fun fact makes it stick ("A year on Mercury lasts 88 days").
  • Wrong is never a punishment: "So close!" and a second go at the same topic.
  • Again reads the question once more; Back revisits an earlier question.

Six levels that grow with your child

  • Level 1: 30 seconds per question and six mistakes allowed. Level 6: 10 seconds and no mistakes.
  • Higher levels bring harder questions first; a topic is passed only within the mistake rule.
  • All 24 topics passed? The next level opens automatically.
  • Maths grows too: from adding and subtracting to 10 up to times tables, division, fractions and percentages.

Memory, language and listening

  • Memo trains memory with pictures from the worlds: 8 to 14 pairs.
  • Everything is read aloud in three languages — ideal for early readers and for a second language.
  • The knowledge-card collection shows what your child already knows, per world and topic.
Who it is for

From pre-school to the last year of primary

Ages 4–6

Not reading yet? No problem: Milo or Luna reads everything. Level 1 with plenty of time, counting dots for sums, Memo with pictures.

Ages 7–9

Reading and listening, collecting facts and cards, the first times tables in Maths, and a slightly tighter timer.

Ages 10–12

Higher levels with less time and no mistakes, two-step sums, percentages and fractions, and the mixed quiz per world.

Also for teachers and after-school clubs: school year and level can be set per child, and the game needs no account.

Privacy policy

Frequently asked questions

What age is Kwizillo for?

Roughly 4 to 12. Young children play with the voice and level 1; older children pick a higher level with less time and harder questions.

Which languages?

English, Dutch and Brazilian Portuguese — the whole app and every question. Switch any time; progress is kept.

Do I need internet?

All pictures, questions and games are inside the app. Only the voice comes from the internet; without a connection the app keeps playing, silently.

What happens on a wrong answer?

Your child hears the right answer, the explanation and the fact. Too many mistakes in a topic? Play that topic once more — never a punishment, always a second chance.

When is the app out?

Kwizillo is being tested on iPhone now. The App Store release follows; email us and we will let you know.

Privacy

Kwizillo privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Kwizillo is a learning game for children. The app collects no personal data, shows no ads and measures no behaviour. Below is exactly what happens, how long anything is kept and how you erase it all. The same text sits inside the app, in the parent zone under Privacy & safety, in all nine languages.

Who is responsible

Kwizillo is published by Olijve Holding B.V., Hunnenoord 20, 7822 BP Emmen, the Netherlands (Dutch Chamber of Commerce no. 89749685). Questions about privacy? Write to hello@kwizillo.com; we answer within thirty days.

What the app stores: on the device only

  • The name your child types (only to greet them on screen).
  • Language, voice choice, sound settings, school year and level.
  • Progress: quizzes played, correct answers, XP, coins, streak, cards, medals and mascots.
  • Whether Premium is active. That status comes from the App Store (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play (Android); we keep no purchase data.

This sits in the app's local storage on the device. It is not sent to Kwizillo or to anyone else. It stays until you erase it, and it disappears when you delete the app or tap Erase all data in the parent zone.

The reading voice

Milo and Luna speak through the ElevenLabs speech service, which only our own server talks to; that service's key is never in the app. All that is sent is the sentence being read aloud: a question, an answer, a hint, a fact or a fixed game line, never your child's name and never anything else your child types. No account, device id or advertising id goes with it.

The server keeps the audio it made in a cache, so the same sentence need not be made twice. That cache holds game sentences only, no personal data. The IP address of a speech request stays in memory for at most a minute to limit abuse of the service; it is never written to disk and never shared.

What we do not do

  • No ads and no advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics, no third-party trackers, no advertising id, no profiling.
  • No accounts, email addresses or passwords: there is nothing with us to sign up for.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • No cookies.

Premium

Kwizillo is free to play. Premium is a subscription billed through Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), depending on your device, that renews automatically until cancelled; you can cancel at any time in your subscriptions in the App Store or in Google Play. Payment runs entirely through Apple or Google: we see no name, address or payment details and learn only from the device whether the subscription is active. There are no purchases inside the game itself and no currency you buy with real money.

Sharing a score

The "Share your score" button opens the device's own share sheet with a short line (name, level, number of correct answers). The button sits behind a parental gate and sends nothing to Kwizillo; where the text goes next is the grown-up's choice.

Keeping and erasing

  • On the device: stays until you erase it. Parent zone → Privacy & safety → Erase all data removes the name, settings, progress, cards and statistics in one go; deleting the app does the same.
  • Audio cache on the server: spoken game sentences only, with no personal data; we can clear it on request.
  • IP address of a speech request: at most one minute in memory, then gone.
  • We keep no visitor log with IP addresses, and we hold no copy of what is on the device.

Children

Kwizillo is made for primary-school children. Because the app collects no personal data from children, stores none on our servers and shares none, no parental consent is needed for data processing (COPPA, GDPR article 8). What the child types stays on the device. Everything that leaves the game (writing mail, sharing, erasing data) sits behind a parental gate.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and erase personal data. In practice we hold nothing of your child's: the data is on the device and you erase it there yourself, in one tap. If you do have a question or a request, write to hello@kwizillo.com. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

If what the app does changes, we update this policy and change the date at the top. The screen inside the app shows that same date.

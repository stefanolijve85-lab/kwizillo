Privacy

Kwizillo privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

Kwizillo is a learning game for children. We collect as little as possible and share nothing with advertisers. This is exactly what the app does.

What the app stores — on the device only

  • The name your child enters (only to greet them on screen).
  • Language, voice, sound settings, school year and level.
  • Progress: quizzes played, correct answers, XP, coins, streak, cards, medals and buddies.

This lives in the app's local storage on the device. It is never sent to Kwizillo or anyone else and disappears when you delete the app or reset progress in the parent zone.

The voice

Milo and Luna speak through a speech service (ElevenLabs) that our own server calls. Only the text of the question, the answers, the hint and the explanation is sent — never your child's name and no other personal data. The server caches generated audio so the same line is not produced twice.

What we do not do

  • No advertising.
  • No tracking, no third-party analytics, no advertising identifiers.
  • No accounts, no email addresses, no location, no contacts, no camera or microphone.
  • No in-app purchases.

Sharing scores

"Share your score" opens the device's share sheet with a short text (name, level, correct answers). The button sits behind a parental gate and sends nothing to Kwizillo.

Children

Kwizillo is intended for children aged 4 to 12. Because the app collects and shares no personal data, no parental consent for data processing is needed. Settings that change the game sit behind a parental gate.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@kwizillo.com.
